Best Friend includes a full sample editor. You can fix a sample properly before it lands on a
pad instead of working around it afterwards.

**All edits are destructive** — they permanently alter your sample. To keep the original,
duplicate it to an empty slot first, as below.

## Processing

These rewrite the sample itself:

- **Crop** to the part you actually want.
- **Normalize** to bring a quiet sample up.
- **Reverse**.
- **Repitch** — the fastest way to shrink a file and reclaim space on the hardware.
- **Stereo to mono**, flattening a stereo file.
- **Change sample rate**.

You can zoom into the waveform to place an edit precisely, and Best Friend can
**automatically detect and set the key** of a sample.

## Effects

Stack any of these on a sample:

- Three-band **EQ**
- **1176-style compression**
- **Distortion** and **saturation**
- **Octaver**
- **Small Stone-style Phaser**
- **Juno-style Chorus**
- **Tremolo**
- **Delay**
- **Reverb**

## Split a sample into stems

Any sample can be split into isolated **drums, bass, vocals, guitar, and instruments**, then
remixed or muted before it reaches a pad. See
[Split a song into stems](/docs/split-a-song-into-stems).

## Replace a sample in place

Edit a sample and put it back on the pad it came from. The arrangement around it is
untouched.

## Copy or duplicate a sample

Change the slot number to one that is empty and save. The edit lands on the new slot and the
original is left as it was.
