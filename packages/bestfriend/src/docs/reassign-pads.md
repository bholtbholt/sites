Rearranging a kit on the hardware means reassigning slots by hand. In Best Friend you drag
the pad.

1. Long-press a filled pad in the grid until it lifts.
2. Drag it onto another pad and release.

Both cells update the instant the drop lands. Drop onto a **filled** pad and the two swap;
drop onto an **empty** one and the sample moves there.

## Across groups

Lift a pad in group A, switch to group B while still holding it, and drop. The pad crosses
groups in one gesture. The switch can happen from the hardware or within the app.

## Unassign a pad

Drag a pad to the trash to unassign its sample. The sample stays in its slot on the hardware, so
you can load it again from the [Samples tab](/docs/organise-your-samples).

## Copy instead of move

To put the same pad in two places, use **Copy Mode** on the [pad grid](/docs/see-a-bank-of-pads):
tap the pad to copy, then the pad to copy it to. The target takes the sample and every setting.

## Settings travel with the sample

Amp, pan, envelope, and Play Mode all move with the pad — a swap gives you the same
sound in a new place, not a sample with somebody else's settings on it.

## Undo

The undo banner appears as soon as the drop lands, and **both** cells flip back when you tap
it. You do not have to be looking at the grid for it to work: switch tabs, come back, and the
restored layout is there.

The undo finishes in a frame, so there is no progress HUD — the banner just dismisses.

### On the EP-40 Riddim

Supertone pads swap like any other pad, in both directions.
