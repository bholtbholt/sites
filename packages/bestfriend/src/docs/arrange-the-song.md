A Song on the EP is a chain of scenes, and building one on the hardware means stepping through
positions one at a time. The **Song** tab on the [Scenes timeline](/docs/see-scenes-and-songs)
lists the whole chain, one row per position.

Edits are staged like any other Scenes edit, then written when you tap **Commit**. See
[edit notes in your Scenes](/docs/edit-notes-in-scenes).

## Edit the chain

- Drag a row to reorder it.
- Swipe a row to duplicate or delete it.
- Tap a row's scene number to pick another scene.
- Tap **+** at the top to add a copy of the last scene.

## Hear it

Choose **Song** from the scene menu to see the whole chain end to end — see
[song mode](/docs/song-mode). On the hardware, hold <kbd>MAIN</kbd> + <kbd>PLAY</kbd> to play the
chain.
