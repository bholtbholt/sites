The [Scenes timeline](/docs/see-scenes-and-songs) is also an editor. Add, move, lengthen, and
erase notes across several pads at once, then write the result to the project in one go.

## Commit or cancel

The EP can't be edited live, so Best Friend stages your changes and writes them when you're
ready. While edits are pending, the main navigation is replaced by **Commit**, **Cancel**,
**Undo**, and **Redo**.

- **Commit** writes your edits to the project.
- **Cancel** throws them away.

Changed your mind after committing? Tap **Undo** on the banner to take the commit back.

A pattern used by several scenes changes in all of them, the same as on the hardware.

**The hardware is the source of truth.** Notes you recorded on the EP since the last re-sync are
kept. If an edit clashes with them, only the clashing edits are dropped.

Switching scenes keeps your edits, so you can commit several scenes together. Changing project on
the hardware asks whether to commit or discard them first.

## Pick a tab

The tabs at the top of the bottom panel choose what you edit:

| Tab   | What it edits                                                                       |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Notes | Notes across several pads                                                           |
| Fader | Fader automation — see [draw fader automation](/docs/draw-fader-automation)         |
| Pitch | The active pad's pitch, as a classic piano roll                                     |
| Step  | The active pad's pattern, as steps — see [the step sequencer](/docs/step-sequencer) |
| Song  | The Song List — see [arrange the song](/docs/arrange-the-song)                      |

Pitch and Step follow the pad selected on the hardware. Press another pad to change.

## Pick a tool

The tools sit beside the scene menu at the top.

- **Cursor** adds, selects, and edits notes.
- **Erase** removes notes you tap.
- **Lasso** selects several notes at once and sets a loop region for previewing.

## Add and change notes

With Cursor:

1. Tap an empty spot to add a note. It takes the length and velocity of the last note you shaped.
2. Tap a note to select it. New notes are selected automatically.
3. Drag a selected note sideways to change its length.
4. Touch and hold a selected note until it lifts, then drag to move it.

Dragging notes up or down between pads is intentionally blocked in Notes, so a hat never lands on
the kick by accident.

## Velocity, pitch, and position

Select a note, then drag its position, pitch, or velocity in the bottom panel up or down. The
arrows on the right move it: the outer pair jumps to the next grid line, the inner pair nudges it.

With several notes selected, every note changes by the same amount.

## Select several notes

With Lasso, drag a box around the notes. Switch to Cursor to drag what you picked, or Erase to
delete it.

Tap a pad in the pad column to cycle through common selections: all notes, on-beats, and
off-beats.

## Edit pitch

The **Pitch** tab shows the active pad on a keyboard from C1 to C8.

- Touch and hold a note until it lifts, then drag up or down to change its pitch, or sideways to
  move it.
- Tap a key to select its notes. Tap again to narrow by beat.

Set a key and scale in the quantize sheet to mark the keys in it.

## Quantize

Tap the quantize button in the bottom panel to set the grid, swing, and nudge. Turn off **Snap to
grid** to place notes freely.

Quantize applies to notes you add, move, or lengthen. Notes already recorded stay where they are.

## Hear edits before you commit

While edits are pending, **Bounce** becomes **Play**. Tap it to hear the edits on your phone. With
notes selected, only those notes play. Use Lasso to set a loop region.

## Bigger rows

Turn on **Large Scenes Grid** in [app settings](/docs/app-settings) to make rows 50% taller in
Notes, Fader, and Pitch, so notes are easier to tap.
