Almost every connection problem with Best Friend is a cable problem. The EP-133 K.O. II,
EP-40 Riddim, and EP-1320 Medieval all connect the same way, so this applies whichever one
you own.

## You need a USB-C **data** cable

A USB-C to USB-C cable that carries **data**, not just power. Many USB-C cables are wired for
charging only. They physically fit and they will charge the EP, which is exactly why they are
so confusing: the hardware lights up, so the cable looks fine, but no data ever crosses and
Best Friend never sees the device.

How to tell:

- **Check the packaging or product listing.** A data cable advertises a transfer speed —
  "USB 2.0", "480 Mbps", "USB 3.2", "10 Gbps". A charge-only cable advertises wattage and
  says nothing about speed.
- **Test it.** Connect a phone to a computer with the cable. If the computer offers to browse
  the phone's files, the cable carries data.
- **Prefer the cable that came with a phone, laptop, or drive.** Those are data cables.

Any data rate is enough. Best Friend does not need a fast cable, it needs a real one.

## USB-C device to USB-C EP

Plug it straight in. Nothing else required.

## Lightning iPhone or iPad

You need two things:

1. An **MFi-certified Lightning to USB adapter** (Apple's Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter
   is the reliable one).
2. A **USB-IF certified USB-A to USB-C cable** between the adapter and the EP.

Uncertified adapters are the most common failure point here. If yours is working
intermittently, that is usually why.

## Mac

A USB-C to USB-C data cable into any port. Best Friend on the Mac requires Apple silicon
(M1 or later).

## Android

A USB-C to USB-C data cable, plus **USB OTG support** on your Android device. OTG lets your
phone act as the host rather than the peripheral — without it the connection is power only.
Most Android devices from the last several years support it.

## Hubs and docks

A powered USB-C hub generally works, but it is one more thing that can be at fault. If you
are diagnosing a problem, take the hub out of the chain first.
