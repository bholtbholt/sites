The **Samples** tab shows every filled slot on your EP, with each sample's name and size. It is
where you reorganise the device rather than the app — everything here writes straight to the
hardware, and every destructive action has an **Undo**.

## Browse

- **iPhone**: scroll the list, or tap the jump button in the bottom right to go to a bank. The list
  follows the active pad. Tap ▶ to hear a sample, and tap a row to
  [edit it](/docs/sample-editor-and-effects).
- **iPad and Mac**: banks are arranged in columns, with keyboard support. Arrow keys move between
  samples, <kbd>Return</kbd> opens the editor, and <kbd>Space</kbd> toggles preview, so samples
  play as you move.

You can search for any sample name.

## One sample at a time

Long-press a sample:

- **Load** assigns it to the active pad. The menu names the pad it will land on — `A7`, for
  instance — and the pad takes the sample's own defaults: envelope, Pitch, Pan, and Play Mode. Undo
  restores both the previous sample **and** its settings.
- **Move** puts it in another slot.
- **Duplicate** copies it to another slot.
- **Rename** changes the name, up to 16 characters.
- **Share** sends it to Files, AirDrop, or another app.
- **Delete** removes it from the hardware. You can also swipe a row left and choose Delete.
- **Convert to multi-sample** turns it into a multi-sample. EP-40 and EP-1320 only. See
  [build a multi-sample](/docs/build-multi-samples).

## Select mode

Tap **Select** in the toolbar menu, then pick as many slots as you like. On iPad and Mac,
<kbd>Space</kbd> selects the current sample and <kbd>Shift</kbd> + arrow keys extend the selection.

- **Move**: drag the selection to new slots. The drop highlight shows the slots it is about to fill —
  in the accent colour if the selection fits from there, in the danger colour if it does not.
- **Delete**: tap the Delete icon in the bottom toolbar. Samples Best Friend has already cached can
  be restored from the undo banner, all in one go.
- **Share**: tap the Share icon. You get one WAV per slot; slots that are not cached locally
  download first.
- **Send to Group**: tap the Group icon to put the selected samples on the empty pads of the
  current group.
- **Convert to a Bank Snapshot**: tap the Bank Snapshot icon to make a new
  [Bank Snapshot](/docs/bank-snapshots) from the selection, in the Library.
