The EP records fader moves but gives you no way to edit them afterwards. The **Fader** tab on the
[Scenes timeline](/docs/see-scenes-and-songs) does.

Edits are staged like any other Scenes edit, then written when you tap **Commit**. See
[edit notes in your Scenes](/docs/edit-notes-in-scenes).

## Draw a lane

1. Open the **Fader** tab in the bottom panel.
2. Pick a type from the badges — LVL, HPF, FX, MOD, and so on. See
   [the lanes](/docs/fader-automation-lanes) for the full list.
3. Pick the **Draw** tool, then touch the line and drag to draw.

## Erase a lane

With the **Erase** tool, tap a group to clear its automation for that type.

## Mod wheel and pitch bend

Editing the **MWH** (mod wheel) or **PBN** (pitch bend) types applies to the active pad, not the
whole group.
