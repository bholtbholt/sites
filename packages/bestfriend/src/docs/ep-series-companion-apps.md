## Best Friend

A companion app for all three EP-Series samplers — EP-133 K.O. II, EP-40 Riddim, and EP-1320
Medieval — on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android. It connects over USB-C and covers the pad
editor, a Scene and Song timeline with fader automation, multi-track stem bouncing off the
hardware, a MIDI keyboard with chords and an arpeggiator, a full sample editor with effects,
stem splitting, multi-sample building on the EP-40 and EP-1320, and a Library of Bank
Snapshots, backups, and bounces synced by iCloud on Apple devices. One-time purchase per
store.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/best-friend/id6782250723) ·
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.studio133.bestfriend) ·
[What it does](/)

## EP Sample Tool

The EP Sample Tool is the official web-based utility developed by Teenage Engineering for managing audio on their EP hardware line, requiring a WebMIDI-compatible browser and a direct USB-C connection. It allows users to easily drag and drop audio files to upload, download, or delete samples.

[Web App](https://teenage.engineering/apps/ep-sample-tool)

## Cornerman for K.O. II

A dedicated third-party sample librarian and backup utility for the physical hardware over a direct USB connection. It focuses purely on utility by allowing users to securely upload, download, timestamp, and share device backups.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/cornerman-for-k-o-ii/id6499280264)

## EP-PatchStudio

A companion app for the EP series with an emphasis on sample preparation: multi-sample creation, auto-sampling, batch processing, and converting between project formats.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/ep-patchstudio/id6778625335)

## PunchKit

Designed to speed up music production workflows, this app features offline 12-pad kit assembly, automatic USB audio take recording, and local AI-driven stem separation. It also introduces a "Punch It" live performance mode that allows producers to destructively bake effects like tunable LoFi filters and vinyl artifacts directly into their audio.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6785249847)
