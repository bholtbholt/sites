The **Step** tab on the [Scenes timeline](/docs/see-scenes-and-songs) is a classic step
sequencer for the active pad. It follows the pad selected on the hardware — press another pad to
switch.

Edits are staged like any other Scenes edit, then written when you tap **Commit**. See
[edit notes in your Scenes](/docs/edit-notes-in-scenes).

## The grid

Step shows one bar of the active pad.

- Choose **16** or **32** steps per bar at the top of the grid.
- Page between bars with the arrows.
- Set the pattern's length in bars with the stepper.

## Program steps

- Tap a step to add a note. Tap it again to clear it.
- Double-tap a step to accent it.
- Drag a step up or down to change its velocity.
- Drag a step sideways to ratchet it: 2x, 3x, or 4x. Ratchets are unavailable in 32-step mode.
- Touch and hold a step to select it without changing it. The bottom panel then edits its notes.

## Tools

The tools change how a step is added:

| Tool     | What it does                                                        |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cursor   | Places notes on the grid                                            |
| Humanize | Places notes slightly off the grid, with varied velocity and length |
| Pitch    | Drag a step up or down to change its pitch                          |

Humanize is the quick way to stop a programmed hat sounding like a programmed hat.
