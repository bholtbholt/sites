Best Friend backs your EP up at three different sizes, depending on how much you want to put
back later. Every backup is **named and dated**, and any of them restores in one tap.

## The three kinds

### 1. Bank Snapshot

A single bank. Arrange a bank the way you want it, snapshot it, and recall it later — even
onto a **different EP**. Use it for a kit you will want again.

Bank Snapshots function like a kit builder, a feature popular on other samplers but not available for the EP series natively.

### 2. Project backup

The project you are currently working on. Use it before a set, before a big edit, or before
you start moving samples around in bulk.

### 3. System backup

Everything on the device. The full safety net.

## Restore

1. Open the Library and find the backup by name or date.
2. **Preview the audio** before restoring — Bank Snapshots, Project backups, and System
   backups can all be listened to first, so you are sure it is the one you meant rather than
   restoring and finding out.
3. Tap Restore for Project and System backups, or Load for Bank Snapshots.

You can also **extract a single Project out of a System backup**, which saves restoring the
whole device when you only wanted one thing back — see
[pull one project out of a system backup](/docs/extract-a-project-from-a-system-backup).

## Where backups live

Backups sit in the Library alongside your bounces. On Apple devices, **iCloud keeps
everything in sync** across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac — take a backup on your phone, restore
it from your Mac.

Android backups are stored on the device. Google Play and the App Store are separate
purchases and separate storage; there is no sync between the two ecosystems.

## Importing existing backups

Projects and Systems exported from Teenage Engineering's official EP Sample Tool can be
imported into Best Friend. Tap the import icon from the Library.
