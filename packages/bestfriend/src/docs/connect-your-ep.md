Best Friend connects to your Teenage Engineering EP over a **wired USB-C data connection**.
There is no Bluetooth and no Wi-Fi pairing — the app reads and writes the hardware directly,
which is what makes it possible to see real pad data and bounce real audio.

## What you need

- A Teenage Engineering **EP-133 K.O. II**, **EP-40 Riddim**, or **EP-1320 Medieval**.
- A **USB-C to USB-C data cable**. Charge-only cables will not work — see
  [Which USB-C cable and adapter you need](/docs/cables-and-adapters). Lightning devices need a **MFi Lightning Adapter** with a **USB-IF certified USB-A to USB-C cable**.
- iOS 26, iPadOS 26, or macOS 26 or later, or Android 12 or later. A Mac needs Apple silicon
  (M1 or later). An Android device needs USB OTG support.

Keep your EP on the latest firmware.

## Connect

1. Power on your EP.
2. Plug the USB-C data cable into the EP and into your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Android device.
3. Open Best Friend. It detects the connected hardware and identifies which EP it is.

There is no pairing step and no permission dialog to approve. If the cable carries data, the
EP appears.

Once connected, Best Friend loads the project currently on the hardware — every pad, every
bank, and the Scene and Song data behind them.

## On a Lightning iPhone or iPad

Older iPhones and iPads use Lightning rather than USB-C. They still work, but they need an
adapter: an **MFi-certified Lightning to USB adapter** together with a **USB-IF certified
USB-A to USB-C cable**. Uncertified adapters are the single most common reason a Lightning
device fails to see the hardware.

## On Android

Your Android device needs **USB OTG** (On-The-Go) support to act as the host on the
connection. Most Android phones and tablets from the last several years have it. If yours
does not, the EP will charge from the connection but will not appear in Best Friend.
