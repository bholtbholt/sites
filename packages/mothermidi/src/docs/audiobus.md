## Playing an instrument

1. Open the **MIDI** tab in Audiobus.
2. Set the **Sender** to **Mother MIDI** and the **Receiver** to your instrument.

## Sending clock

1. Turn on clock sending in Audiobus's **MIDI Clock** settings.
2. Add **Mother MIDI** as a destination.
3. Press play.
