---
title: Connecting to a Mac
summary: 'Connect your iPhone or iPad to a Mac with a cable and turn on Inter-Device Audio and MIDI.'

Play and record Mother MIDI in a DAW on your Mac, and run the arpeggiator from the DAW's clock.

A cable alone isn't enough. Turn on Inter-Device Audio and MIDI:

1. Connect your iPhone or iPad to the Mac with a cable. Unlock it, and tap **Trust** if asked.
2. Open Mother MIDI.
3. On the Mac, open **Audio MIDI Setup** and choose **Window > Show Audio Devices**.
4. Select your iPhone or iPad and click **Enable**.

Your DAW lists a MIDI port named after your device, such as "Brian's iPhone", not "Mother MIDI".

If you picked destinations by hand, turn on **IDAM MIDI Host** in the MIDI tab of Settings.

Unplugging the cable or restarting the Mac drops the connection. Click **Enable** again. If the port shows in Audio MIDI Setup but not in your DAW, restart the DAW.
