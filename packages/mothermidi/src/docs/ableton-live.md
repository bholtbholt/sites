[Connect to the Mac](/docs/connecting-to-a-mac) first.

## Playing and recording

1. Open **Live > Settings** and choose **Link, Tempo & MIDI**.
2. Under MIDI Ports, turn on **Track** for your device's **In** row.
3. Create a MIDI track with an instrument and arm it.

## Sending clock

1. Turn on **Sync** for your device's **Out** row.
2. Press play.

If the arpeggio drifts ahead or behind, adjust that row's **MIDI Clock Sync Delay**.
