## Playing and recording

1. Open **Live > Settings** and choose **Link, Tempo & MIDI**.
2. Under MIDI Ports, find the **In** row for your iPhone or iPad and turn on **Track**.
3. Create a MIDI track with an instrument and arm it.

## Sending clock

1. In the same MIDI Ports list, find the **Out** row for your iPhone or iPad and turn on **Sync**.
2. Press play.

If the arpeggio runs slightly ahead of or behind Live, adjust **MIDI Clock Sync Delay** on that row's settings.
