The arpeggiator plays the held chord one note at a time, in time with an incoming MIDI clock. Mother MIDI doesn't make a clock of its own. With no clock coming in, the arpeggiator is silent. Send clock from your DAW or hardware to Mother MIDI. See [Syncing the arpeggiator](/docs/syncing-the-arpeggiator).

Toggle the arpeggiator button in the toolbar, then play a chord. The Arp tab of Settings shows which clock it is following.

Three quick controls sit beside the Pitch wheel. Tap one to step through its options:

- **Rate**: 4, 8, 8T, 16, 16T, 32.
- **Mode**: Up, Down, Up-Down, Down-Up.
- **Oct**: 1, 2 or 3 octaves.

The Arp tab of Settings has these controls too, plus:

- **Root +12** adds the root an octave above the top note.
- **Double notes** plays every step twice.
