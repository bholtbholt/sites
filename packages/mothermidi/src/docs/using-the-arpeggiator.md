The arpeggiator plays the held chord one note at a time. It needs a MIDI clock, from your DAW or from Mother MIDI's own tempo. See [Syncing the arpeggiator](/docs/syncing-the-arpeggiator).

Tap the arpeggiator in the toolbar, then play a chord.

## Quick controls

Tap a control beside the Pitch wheel to step through it:

- **Rate**: 4, 8, 8T, 16, 16T, and 32.
- **Mode**: Up, Down, Up-Down, and Down-Up.
- **Oct**: 1, 2, or 3 octaves.

## The Arp tab

The Arp tab in Settings shows the same controls, plus:

- **Root +12** adds the root an octave above the top note.
- **Double notes** plays every step twice.
