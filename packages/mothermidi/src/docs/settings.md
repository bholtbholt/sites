The Settings tab in Settings sets how the keys respond to your touch.

## Split Keyboard into Zones

Turn on **Root position**, **1st Inversion**, and **2nd Inversion** to split each key into blocks that invert the chord. See [Playing chords and extensions](/docs/playing-chords-and-extensions).

## Velocity

**Velocity sensitive** plays louder at the top of a key. Drag the slider to set the softest and loudest notes. With it off, notes play at 100.

## Modulation

**Modulation sensitive** raises modulation as you drag up on a held key. Drag the slider to set its range.
