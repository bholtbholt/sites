---
title: Choosing where notes go
summary: 'Send notes to every connected destination, or only the ones you pick.'
section: playing
order: 10
---

Notes go to every connected destination by default, including gear you plug in later. Destinations are detected automatically, and each can be turned on and off by hand in the MIDI tab of Settings.

Mother MIDI also shows up in other apps as a MIDI source called **Mother MIDI**, so a DAW or a host like [AUM](/docs/aum) on the same device can always record it. To use a DAW on a Mac, see [Connecting to a Mac](/docs/connecting-to-a-mac).
