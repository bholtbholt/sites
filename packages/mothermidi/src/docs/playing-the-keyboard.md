Touch one of the 12 keys to play a note. Drag the bar under the keyboard to shift the range, and use the Octave Stepper to change octaves. Tap **Sub** to add a suboctave of the root.

Only one key can be pressed at a time.

## Velocity and modulation

The keyboard responds to velocity and modulation. Both settings are in the Settings tab of Settings.

**Velocity sensitive** is on by default. Touch the top of a key to play louder and the bottom to play quieter. Set the softest and loudest values with the range slider (default 80–127). With it off, every note plays at 100.

**Modulation sensitive** is off by default. Drag up on a held key to raise modulation and down to lower it. Modulation stays where you left it between notes. Turning the setting off resets modulation to zero.
