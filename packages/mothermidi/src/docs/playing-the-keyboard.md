Touch a key to play its note. One key plays at a time.

## Changing the range

Drag the bar under the keyboard to shift the range. Use the **Octave** stepper to change octaves. Tap **Sub** to add the root an octave down.

## Velocity and modulation

**Velocity sensitive** is on by default. Touch the top of a key to play louder, the bottom to play quieter.

**Modulation sensitive** is off by default. Drag up on a held key to raise modulation, down to lower it.

Set both in [Settings](/docs/settings).

## Bending the pitch

Drag the **Pitch** wheel. It springs back when you let go. The bend range is set on your instrument.
