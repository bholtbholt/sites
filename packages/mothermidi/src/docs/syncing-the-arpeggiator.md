The arpeggiator needs a MIDI clock. Chords and single notes don't.

Tap the tempo in the toolbar, or open the MIDI tab in Settings.

## Following a DAW or instrument

Choose **External** to follow the clock sent to Mother MIDI. Each DAW guide shows how to send it.

- Most DAWs send clock only while playing. Press play, then play a chord.
- To jam without recording, loop the DAW and leave it running.

The toolbar reads **No clock** when none is arriving.

## Setting the tempo here

Choose **Internal** and set the tempo with the stepper, or tap **Tap** in time.

Turn on **Send clock** so your gear follows the tempo. It never starts or stops their sequencers.
