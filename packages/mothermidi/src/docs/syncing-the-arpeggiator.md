---
title: Syncing the arpeggiator
summary: "Run the arpeggiator from your DAW's MIDI clock."
section: connecting
order: 80
---

The arpeggiator follows the DAW's MIDI clock. Each DAW guide shows how to send clock to your iPhone or iPad.

- Most DAWs send clock only while playing. Press play, then play a chord.
- To jam without recording, turn on the DAW's loop or cycle and leave it running.
- The Arp tab of Settings shows which clock Mother MIDI is following.

Chords and single notes don't need a clock. Only the arpeggiator does.
