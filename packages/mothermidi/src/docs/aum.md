## Playing an instrument

1. Add an instrument to a channel in AUM.
2. In **MIDI Routing**, connect the **Mother MIDI** source to the instrument.

## Sending clock

1. Turn on **Send MIDI Clock** in AUM's settings.
2. In **MIDI Routing**, connect **AUM Clock** to the **Mother MIDI** destination.
3. Press play.
