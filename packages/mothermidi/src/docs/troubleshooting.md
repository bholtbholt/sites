---
title: Troubleshooting
summary: "What to check when the DAW can't see Mother MIDI, hears nothing from it, or leaves the arpeggiator silent."
section: connecting
order: 130
---

- **The DAW doesn't list the port.** Check Audio MIDI Setup: the iPhone or iPad should show **Enable** turned on. Unlock the device and click Enable again, then restart the DAW.
- **The DAW gets nothing.** In Mother MIDI, open Settings, choose the MIDI tab, and make sure **All destinations** or **IDAM MIDI Host** is on.
- **The arpeggiator is silent.** Press play in the DAW, and check that clock is sent to your iPhone or iPad. The Arp tab of Settings says when clock is coming in.
- **Notes land late.** A cable adds a small, constant delay. Fix it with the DAW's recording delay setting rather than by quantizing.
