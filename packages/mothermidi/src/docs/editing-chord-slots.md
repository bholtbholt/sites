Use the Chords tab in Settings to change what each chord and extension button plays.

## Changing a chord

1. Tap a chord.
2. Tap notes to add or remove them.
3. Tap the checkmark to save.

A chord holds up to 5 notes across two octaves. It is shown from C and plays from whichever key you press.

## Changing an extension

An extension is one note. Tap it, pick the note, and tap the checkmark.

## Going back to the defaults

Tap **Reset** above a column.
