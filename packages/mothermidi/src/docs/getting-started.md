Use Mother MIDI to play chords and arpeggios on a synth, a sampler, or a DAW. It sends MIDI notes and makes no sound on its own.

## Connecting an instrument

1. Connect the instrument over USB, or open a music app on the same device.
2. Match the channel in the toolbar to your instrument's.
3. Play a key.

To play into a DAW on a computer, see [Connecting to a Mac](/docs/connecting-to-a-mac).

## The toolbar

- **Settings** is on the left.
- The tempo and channel sit in the centre. Tap either to change it.
- The **arpeggiator** is on the right.

## Seeing the walkthrough again

In Settings, choose the Settings tab and tap **See onboarding tutorial**.
